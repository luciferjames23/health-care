04 / MODELS & PRIORITY RULES
DenseNet one twenty one screens for opacity at a twenty percent probability threshold. YOLO eleven nano localizes opacity at ten percent confidence or above. Both models positive means high priority. Exactly one positive means medium priority. Neither model flags opacity means low priority. Low priority still requires radiologist review.
