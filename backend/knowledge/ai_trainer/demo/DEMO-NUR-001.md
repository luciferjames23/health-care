## Purpose
Provide demo guidance for common nursing activities.

## Patient Identification
Before a clinical procedure or medication administration:
1. Verify patient identity using at least two approved identifiers.
2. Compare identification details with the authorised clinical record.
3. Confirm intended procedure or medication.
4. Do not rely only on bed or room number.
5. If information does not match, stop and escalate according to policy.

## Pre-Procedure Verification
Before beginning:
1. Confirm the correct patient.
2. Confirm the authorised procedure.
3. Review relevant allergies or precautions where available.
4. Ensure required equipment is ready.
5. Perform hand hygiene.
6. Use appropriate PPE.
7. Explain the procedure where appropriate.
8. Document completion where required.

## Vital-Sign Monitoring
1. Confirm patient identity.
2. Use approved equipment.
3. Record measurements accurately.
4. Note abnormal readings.
5. Escalate unexpected findings according to applicable protocol.
6. Record time of observation.

## IV Cannula Care
General principles:
- perform hand hygiene
- inspect insertion site
- maintain appropriate aseptic technique
- keep site secure
- document observations
- escalate suspected complications

## Documentation
Clinical documentation should be accurate, timely, legible, complete, and traceable.
