## Hand Hygiene
Typical required situations include:
- before patient contact
- before an aseptic task
- after body-fluid exposure risk
- after patient contact
- after contact with the patient's surroundings

## Gloves
Gloves do not replace hand hygiene.
1. Perform hand hygiene where required.
2. Select appropriate gloves.
3. Use gloves for intended task.
4. Change gloves when required.
5. Remove contaminated gloves safely.
6. Perform hand hygiene after removal where applicable.

## PPE
PPE selection depends on exposure, isolation category, procedure, and hospital policy. Possible PPE includes gloves, gown/apron, mask, respiratory protection, and eye protection.

## Sterile Technique
1. Prepare equipment.
2. Perform hand hygiene.
3. Maintain sterile field.
4. Avoid contact between sterile and non-sterile surfaces.
5. Replace contaminated items.
6. Follow relevant SOP.

## Suspected Breach
If sterile technique is breached:
- stop where appropriate
- correct breach
- replace contaminated material
- re-establish sterile technique
- escalate if required
- document if required
