## Medication Administration Checks
Before medication administration verify according to hospital policy:
1. Correct patient
2. Correct medication
3. Correct prescribed dose
4. Correct route
5. Correct time
6. Correct documentation
7. Relevant allergies
8. Required monitoring/precautions

If information is incomplete or inconsistent, do not proceed until clarified through the authorised clinical process.

## High-Alert Medications
Before administration:
1. Verify authorised medication order.
2. Confirm medication name and prescribed dose.
3. Confirm route and administration instructions.
4. Review approved preparation/dilution reference where required.
5. Perform required independent double-check.
6. Confirm monitoring requirements.
7. Document administration.

## Drug Dilution Questions
AG-13 must not invent dilution instructions. Search the approved medication/pharmacy SOP, verify document status/version, and display the relevant section and citation. If no approved instruction exists: “No current approved hospital dilution instruction was found in the available knowledge base. Please verify with the authorised pharmacy or responsible clinician before proceeding.”

## Two-Person Verification
Where policy requires an independent checker, verification must be performed independently.

## Medication Discrepancy
If medication information appears inconsistent: stop, do not guess, clarify through the authorised pathway, and document where required.
