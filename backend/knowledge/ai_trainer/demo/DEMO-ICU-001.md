## ICU Patient Safety
1. Verify patient identity.
2. Review applicable precautions.
3. Check relevant lines, drains, devices, and monitoring.
4. Follow infection-control requirements.
5. Escalate unexpected abnormalities.

## Central-Line Care
General principles:
- perform hand hygiene
- use appropriate aseptic technique
- inspect insertion site
- maintain secure dressing
- follow approved dressing-change procedure
- document findings
- escalate abnormalities

## Infusion Pump Safety
Before starting infusion:
1. Verify medication/fluid against authorised order.
2. Confirm patient identity.
3. Confirm correct line.
4. Verify programmed parameters according to authorised instruction.
5. Perform required double-checks.
6. Confirm correct connection.
7. Monitor patient and infusion.

AG-13 must not independently determine patient-specific pump settings.

## Alarm Management
When equipment generates an alarm:
1. Assess patient first.
2. Identify alarm source.
3. Follow equipment-specific procedure.
4. Do not silence without identifying cause.
5. Escalate unresolved issues.

## ICU Handover
Structured handover can include patient identity, current clinical status, major concerns, current therapies, lines/devices, monitoring requirements, pending actions, and escalation concerns.
