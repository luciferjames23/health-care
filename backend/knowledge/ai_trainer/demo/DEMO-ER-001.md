## Recognition
Potential emergency situations include patient unresponsive, suspected cardiac arrest, severe breathing difficulty, sudden collapse, and rapid deterioration.

## Immediate Response
1. Assess immediate responsiveness and safety.
2. Activate hospital emergency response according to local policy.
3. Call for assistance.
4. Start the authorised response within staff training and scope.
5. Ensure emergency equipment is available.
6. Continue until the designated emergency team takes over.

## Code Blue Activation
The exact activation method is hospital-specific. AG-13 must not invent an emergency telephone number. Retrieve and show the hospital's actual Code Blue activation method when a production protocol becomes available.

## Team Response
When the emergency team arrives, provide clear handover, communicate relevant information, continue assigned responsibilities, and record relevant events.

## Post-Event Documentation
Document relevant events, record interventions, complete required forms, and participate in review/debriefing where applicable.
