# Blood Transfusion Safety SOP

## 1. Purpose

This synthetic demonstration protocol supports safe blood transfusion workflow retrieval, citation, search, and training. It is not approved for live patient-care use. Follow current hospital-approved policies and procedures.

## Page 2

## 2. Pre-Transfusion Verification

Before starting a blood transfusion, verify the patient's identity using the approved identification process. Confirm the authorised transfusion order and required compatibility checks. Check the blood component label against the intended patient and authorised documentation. Review relevant allergies, previous transfusion reactions, and precautions. If any information does not match, stop and escalate according to hospital policy.

## 3. Independent Double-Check

Where hospital policy requires it, two authorised staff members should independently verify the patient, blood component, compatibility details, and prescribed transfusion information before initiation. The second checker should perform an independent verification rather than simply agreeing with the first checker.

## 4. Monitoring During Transfusion

Observe the patient according to the approved hospital monitoring protocol. Record the required observations and vital signs. Remain alert for unexpected symptoms or signs of a possible reaction, and follow the approved hospital procedure if concerns arise.

## 5. Suspected Transfusion Reaction

If a transfusion reaction is suspected, follow the hospital's approved transfusion-reaction procedure immediately. Do not rely on this synthetic demonstration document for patient-specific treatment instructions.

## 6. Documentation

Record the blood component details, relevant verification, required monitoring observations, and whether the transfusion was completed or discontinued. Document any adverse event and escalation using the authorised incident-reporting and transfusion-reaction workflows. Preserve the required documentation and materials according to hospital procedure.
